## 2. DECISION TREE — FOLLOWERS

| Outcome | Bulan 2 strategy |
|---|---|
| **>= 1.000** (target tercapai) | Lanjut velocity sama. Tambah 1 platform pendukung (LinkedIn weekly post). |
| **500-999** (close miss) | Audit hook bank — Bulan 2 fokus 50% Confession + Question (lebih sustainable engagement). |
| **< 500** (significant miss) | **Diagnosis dulu** sebelum action: voice slip? slot timing? hook saturate? — JANGAN ganti pillar. |
| **> 1.500** (overshoot) | Lock voice + format yang berhasil. Hindari godaan ekspansi. Bulan 2 fokus: tambah depth, bukan reach. |

### Anti-pattern

- "Belum cukup followers" -> tambah TikTok / pivot ke viral content. **TIDAK.** Voice tenang ≠ TikTok hype-driven.
- "Sudah 1.000 followers" -> langsung launch produk berbayar. **TIDAK.** Section 17 STRATEGI: trust-first sequencing.

## 3. DECISION TREE — EMAIL OPT-IN

| Outcome | Bulan 2 strategy |
|---|---|
| **>= 200** (target tercapai) | Activate newsletter reguler (1x/minggu). Build content pipeline newsletter Bulan 2. |
| **100-199** (close miss) | Newsletter activate juga, tapi cadence 1x/2 minggu — tidak rush content. |
| **< 100** (significant miss) | **Audit Lead Magnet:** judul lemah? LP friction? CTA placement? — drop newsletter Bulan 2, fokus fix funnel dulu. |
| **> 400** (overshoot) | Newsletter 2x/minggu. Test tier upgrade (free -> "founding member" donation tier?). |

### Newsletter Bulan 2 — kalau activate

- Cadence: Senin pagi 09:00 WIB (sebelum slot Threads)
- Format: long-form essay 600-1.000 kata (3-4x lipat email H1-H7 Lead Magnet)
- Voice sama dengan Threads, tapi depth lebih
- Subject convention: "[topic] / [number]" (consecutive numbering) — bukan clickbait
- Repurpose Threads top post Bulan 1 jadi newsletter Bulan 2 minggu 1-4 -> no extra writing burden

## 4. DECISION TREE — ENGAGEMENT %

| Outcome | Bulan 2 strategy |
|---|---|
| **>= 5%** (sehat) | Lanjut hook style yang terbukti. Bulan 2 = consolidation, bukan eksplorasi. |
| **3-4.9%** (borderline) | Rule #1 trigger akan terjadi. Bulan 2 ganti hook style 50%: kurangi Contrarian, tambah Confession + Question. |
| **< 3%** (Rule #1 active) | Bulan 2 = **stop posting 3 hari** (Day 31-33), restart dengan filter ketat. Diagnosis voice atau topik mismatch. |
| **> 8%** (very high) | Lock format. Audit pillar dominasi (mungkin terlalu P3-heavy yang naturally engage tinggi tapi tidak monetize). |

---

## 5. DECISION TREE — ICP MATCH %

| Outcome | Bulan 2 strategy |
|---|---|
| **>= 30%** (sehat) | Lanjut — audience Anda yang benar. |
| **15-29%** | Audit hook 2 minggu. Apakah hook menarik audience yang salah? Bulan 2 tighten hook bank: drop hook yang attract pelajar/curious-only. |
| **< 15%** (Rule #7 active) | **Stop hook style yang dominant Bulan 1.** Bulan 2 fokus eksklusif Pillar 1 (MONETIZE) + tighter language (decision-maker, pemimpin, profesional). |
