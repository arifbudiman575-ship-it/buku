## Section 0 — Ringkasan Operasional Corporate Inbound

### 0.1. Activation Timeline

| Periode | Status corporate inbound | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Bulan 1-3 (Mgg 1-12)** | TIDAK aktif promosi corporate. Fokus = personal brand + buku launch + cohort launch | Email saved replies basic (auto-defer ke Bulan 4) |
| **Bulan 4 (Mgg 13-16)** | **SOFT activation.** Cohort 1 jalan, 1-pager publik di featured LinkedIn + arifb.id/corporate page live | Audience yang DM = qualified flow Section 3 |
| **Bulan 5-6 (Mgg 17-26)** | **FULL activation.** Cohort 1 selesai, testimonial harvest done, case study composite ready | Active outreach decision-maker (LinkedIn connection request 5-10/mgg) |
| **Bulan 7+ (Mgg 27+)** | **STEADY state.** 2-3 corporate audit/bulan target, capacity ~3 simultaneous engagements | Refresh quarterly per STRATEGY-REFRESH §6 |

**Kenapa defer Bulan 4+:**
- Mgg 1-12 = focus build authority (buku + cohort = trust signal yang dibutuhkan corporate decision-maker)
- Corporate decision cycle 2-6 bulan — lead masuk Mgg 1 → close Bulan 4-5 anyway
- Premature corporate push = signal "saya butuh client cepat" = trust break

### 0.2. 3-Scope Corporate Audit (FINAL)

| Scope | Harga | Tim size target | Format | Slot/Bulan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Small** | Rp 25.000.000 | 5-15 orang (1 fungsi tunggal) | 4 sesi diagnostic + 1 deliverable + 1 handover | 2 max |
| **Medium** | Rp 50.000.000 | 15-30 orang (2-3 fungsi) | 4 sesi diagnostic + 1 deliverable + 1 handover | 2 max |
| **Large** | Rp 100.000.000 | 30-50 orang (cross-functional) | 6 sesi diagnostic + 1 deliverable strategic + 2 handover | 1 max |

- **Range publik** (Rp 25-100JT) di 1-pager + LinkedIn featured + arifb.id/corporate page

## Section 1 — Aturan SACRED Corporate Inbound

7 aturan **non-negotiable**. High-ticket B2B = brand risk lebih tinggi dari cohort. Slip = nuke 1 client + reputational damage di network corporate decision-maker (network kecil, gosip cepat).

| # | Aturan | Rasional |
|---|---|---|
| **CO-1** | **Tidak quote exact price publik.** Range Rp 25-100JT OK publik. Exact quote = post-discovery call 30-min only. | Exact price publik = decision-maker yang tidak fit budget akan push lower. Range = self-filter. |
| **CO-2** | **Discovery call 30-min wajib sebelum scoping.** Tidak skip ke proposal langsung walaupun klien push. | Scope tanpa discovery = high refund/dispute risk. Discovery = filter qualification + scope clarity. |
| **CO-3** | **Anti-scope-creep SACRED di contract.** 3 revision rounds max per deliverable. Beyond = additional engagement. | Scope creep = engagement berlarut + Anti-Burnout S-4 violation + reputational risk delivery delay. |
| **CO-4** | **NDA by request, bukan auto-offer.** Decision-maker yang butuh NDA akan minta. | Auto-offer NDA = signal "kami biasa handle sensitive" yang premature di Bulan 4. By request = trust signal mature. |
| **CO-5** | **Tidak terima engagement yang Anda tidak yakin bisa deliver.** Decline kalau scope di luar A.R.S.I. expertise (mis: pure tech infrastructure, AI engineering deep). | Bad-fit engagement = quality drop = nuke reputation di network corporate. Better decline + refer ke vendor lain. |
| **CO-6** | **Payment 50/50 SACRED** — 50% kickoff + 50% handover. Tidak negosiasi 30/70 atau pure post-pay. | Cash flow stability + signal komitmen klien. Post-pay = high collection risk + power imbalance. |
| **CO-7** | **Klien data privacy SACRED selamanya.** Tidak pakai detail klien untuk konten editorial tanpa consent eksplisit + anonimisasi composite. | Privacy slip = network corporate decision-maker boycott. 1 slip = 5-10 lost potential client (gosip propagasi). |

**Kalau salah satu CO-1 sampai CO-7 dilanggar:**
- CO-1/4/6: Recover via apology + adjust + lessons learned doc
- CO-2/5: STOP engagement, refund (kalau pre-kickoff) atau exit clean (kalau mid-engagement)
- CO-3: Renegotiate scope tertulis, kalau klien tidak terima = escalate ke exit clean
- CO-7: Critical breach. STOP all corporate inbound 30 hari, audit data handling, restart dengan reset SOP

### 1.1. Yang TIDAK BOLEH bocor (sacred ke corporate engagement)

- **Detail klien specific** (nama perusahaan, lokasi, tim size exact, financial data) → tidak share publik tanpa consent
- **Tools internal yang dipakai PROMIND-NEUROLINK + saya** → di proposal hanya kategori (mis: "AI workflow optimization tooling")
- **Methodology kustom yang dibuild untuk klien** → kontent editorial generic OK, detail spesifik klien = NDA
- **Pricing klien lain** → tidak pernah disclosed walaupun ditanya ("klien sebelumnya bayar berapa?")

### 1.2. Yang TIDAK BOLEH ada di copy 1-pager / LinkedIn corporate

- **Promise spesifik angka revenue** ("ROI 10x dalam 6 bulan") — tidak ada studi statistik untuk justify, hype claim
- **Testimonial palsu atau exaggerated** — anchor §17 trust-first
- **Logo klien tanpa consent** — anchor SACRED CO-7 privacy
- **"Limited spots / urgency" framing** — corporate B2B reject hype framing
- **Comparison vs kompetitor by name** — premium positioning = sendirian di kategori

### 1.3. Yang HARUS muncul di copy corporate

- **Range pricing transparent** (Rp 25-100JT) + scope matrix link
- **Refund/no-refund commitment explicit** (no refund post-kickoff, scope-bounded contract)
- **Methodology signature** (A.R.S.I. branded sebagai differentiator)
- **Author authority** (buku THE AI ARCHITECT + 31 audit klien track record)
- **Anonimisasi case study** (kalau ada — mis: "Founder, services profesional, tim 25 orang")
- **Discovery call CTA** (gratis 30-min, tanpa pitch)

### 1.4. Aturan eksklusif untuk Bulan 4-6 (SOFT to FULL activation)

- **Decline rate ≥30% expected** dari inbound — Bulan 4-6 = filter aggressive, decline yang tidak fit (tim <5 orang, decision-maker bukan otoritas, budget <Rp 20JT)
- **Discovery call response time** target 24 jam business hours (bukan 30-min seperti cohort launch)
- **Proposal turnaround time** 5-7 hari kerja post-scoping (bukan instant — quality > speed)
- **Energy cap:** 2 corporate engagement aktif max di Bulan 4 (build capacity gradual). Mgg 13-16 = onboarding cohort 1 + 1-on-1 PLUS = sudah heavy load

## Section 3 — Inbound Qualification Flow

### 3.1. End-to-End Flow

```
[Inbound trigger]
   │
   ├─→ LinkedIn DM
   ├─→ Email corporate@arifb.id
   ├─→ Form arifb.id/corporate
   ├─→ Threads/IG DM (rare untuk corporate)
   └─→ Cohort alumni referral
   │
   ▼
[Lead qualification 24 jam] (saya / VA filter)
   │
   ├─→ Qualified (tim 5+ + decision authority + AI tools 6+ bulan)
   │      │
   │      ▼
   │  [Reply email CO-IN-1 + Calendly discovery 30-min]
   │      │
   │      ▼
   │  [Discovery call 30-min — Section 3.3]
   │      │
   │      ├─→ Fit + budget → Scoping call 60-min booked
   │      │      │
   │      │      ▼
   │      │  [Scoping call 60-min — Section 3.4]
   │      │      │
   │      │      ▼
   │      │  [Proposal sent dalam 5-7 hari kerja]
   │      │      │
   │      │      ├─→ Approve → Contract + 50% upfront → Kickoff
   │      │      ├─→ Negotiate scope → Revision proposal (max 2 round)
   │      │      └─→ Decline → Decline-graceful + add ke nurture list
   │      │
   │      └─→ Not fit → Reply CO-IN-2 (graceful decline + alternative path)
   │
   └─→ Not qualified (tim <5 / no authority / pure curiosity)
          │
          ▼
       [Reply CO-IN-3 graceful redirect ke buku/cohort]
```

### 3.2. Lead Qualification Criteria

| Dimensi | Qualified | Borderline | Not Qualified |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tim size** | 5-50 orang | 3-5 (case-by-case) | <3 atau >50 |
| **Decision authority** | Founder / C-level / VP+ | Manajer senior | IC / individu |
| **AI tools experience** | 6+ bulan team-wide | 3-6 bulan | <3 bulan |
| **Urgency** | 1-3 bulan timeline | 3-6 bulan | >6 bulan / undefined |
| **Budget signal** | Mention budget Rp 20JT+ | Asks for pricing first | Pushes for free consult |
| **ICP match** | Founder Indonesia, services/manufacturing/tech | International (Bulan 4-6 = pass) | Non-business audience |

**Auto-qualified path:** 5+ "Qualified" column + 0 "Not Qualified" → langsung discovery call.
**Manual review:** 3-4 "Qualified" + 1-2 "Borderline" → saya cek personal, putuskan case-by-case.
**Auto-decline:** 2+ "Not Qualified" → CO-IN-3 graceful redirect.

### 3.3. Discovery Call Format (30-min)

**Slot:** 4 slot/minggu via Calendly (Senin/Rabu 14:00-14:30 + Selasa/Kamis 10:00-10:30).

**Agenda 30-min:**

| Menit | Block | Apa yang terjadi |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | Welcome + ground rules | Sapa, jelaskan 30-min ini = diagnostic call gratis (no pitch) |
| 3-15 | **Klien speak** | "Tim Anda — ukuran, fungsi, masalah AI saat ini." Saya banyak listen, sedikit klarifikasi. |
| 15-22 | **Saya analisis** | "Yang saya dengar = pola [X]. Pattern ini biasanya muncul kalau [konteks]. Yang biasanya berhasil di pola ini = [framework refresh A.R.S.I.]" |
| 22-27 | **Fit assessment** | Jujur: cocok corporate audit / cocok cohort dulu / cocok buku / mungkin tidak cocok kerja sama |
| 27-30 | **Next step** | Kalau fit corporate → propose scoping call 60-min minggu depan. Kalau tidak fit → graceful close + alternative path |

**SACRED:**
- TIDAK pitch corporate audit di discovery call walaupun audience ready bayar
- TIDAK quote exact price (CO-1)
- Discovery call = saya lebih banyak listen + diagnose
- Output discovery: yes/no scoping call invitation

### 3.4. Scoping Call Format (60-min)

**Slot:** 2 slot/minggu via Calendly (Selasa 14:00-15:00 + Kamis 14:00-15:00). **Gated** — link Calendly hanya dikirim manual post-discovery call.

**Pre-requisite:** Klien sudah isi pre-scoping form (5 pertanyaan):
1. Tim breakdown — berapa orang per fungsi
2. 3-5 alur paling memakan waktu di tim (specific list)
3. Tools AI yang sudah dipakai + frustrasi spesifik
4. 1 alur yang Anda inginkan kami audit dalam paling fokus (priority 1)
5. Timeline + budget range yang Anda envision

**Agenda 60-min:**

| Menit | Block | Apa yang terjadi |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Welcome + recap discovery | Recap pattern yang sudah teridentifikasi di discovery |
| 5-25 | **Walkthrough alur prioritas** | Klien walkthrough 1 alur priority 1 detail. Saya tanya klarifikasi. |
| 25-40 | **Saya scope & sketsa** | Saya sketsa 4 sesi diagnostic + 1 deliverable yang sesuai konteks. Live whiteboarding (Miro/screen share). |
| 40-50 | **Pricing exact + timeline** | Quote exact (Rp X JT — di range Section 5 matrix). Timeline 6-8 minggu. Payment 50/50. |
| 50-55 | **Q&A klien** | Konteks-specific questions (refund, NDA, IP, tools, dll) |
| 55-60 | **Next step** | Saya kirim proposal tertulis 5-7 hari kerja. Kalau klien yes-in-principle = signal commit. |

**SACRED:**
- Quote exact HANYA di scoping call (CO-1)
- Sketsa 4 sesi diagnostic = ditulis live di Miro/Notion (bukan generic template)
- Klien lihat saya berpikir live = trust signal authenticity

### 3.5. Edge Cases Qualification Flow

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Inbound DM/email tanpa konteks ("info dong tentang corporate audit") | Reply CO-IN-1 + minta 5-pertanyaan basic context (tim, fungsi, urgency) |

## Section 4 — Diagnostic Sesi Format (4-Sesi Standard)

Standard format untuk Small (Rp 25JT) + Medium (Rp 50JT). **Large (Rp 100JT) = 6-sesi variant** dijelaskan Section 4.5.

### 4.1. Sesi 1 — Audit Alur Kerja Real (90-min)

**Tujuan:** Mapping alur kerja tim yang **real** (bukan ideal) + identify 3-5 alur leverage tinggi.

**Pre-sesi (klien siapkan):**
- Org chart tim
- 3-5 alur kerja primary (deskripsi naratif 1-2 paragraf per alur)
- Tools AI yang sudah dipakai + frustrasi spesifik
- 1 alur yang klien inginkan dipriority (dari pre-scoping form)

**Agenda 90-min:**

| Menit | Block |
|---|---|
| 0-10 | Welcome + recap konteks dari scoping call |
| 10-50 | Klien walkthrough 3-5 alur — saya tanya klarifikasi (deep) |
| 50-70 | Saya sketsa diagram alur live (Miro/Notion) — visualize bottleneck + leverage point |
| 70-85 | Identifikasi 3 alur priority untuk audit dalam (sesi 2-4) |
| 85-90 | Action item — klien siapkan data sesi 2 |

**Output sesi 1:** Diagram alur + 3 alur priority list.

### 4.2. Sesi 2-3 — Audit Alur Priority Dalam (90-min × 2)

**Per sesi = 1 alur priority dalam.**

**Pre-sesi (klien siapkan):**
- 1 alur priority detail step-by-step (dari "input" sampai "output")
- Sample input/output real (3-5 contoh)
- Pain point per step (spesifik)
- Time tracking per step kalau ada (estimasi OK)

**Agenda 90-min per sesi:**

| Menit | Block |
|---|---|
| 0-10 | Recap diagram alur dari sesi 1 |
| 10-40 | Klien walkthrough step-by-step alur — saya tanya klarifikasi |
| 40-60 | A.R.S.I. analysis: Audit (waste/leverage), Refine (output standard), Systemize (permission), Integrate (tools fit) |
| 60-80 | Saya sketsa "future state" alur dengan A.R.S.I. applied |
| 80-90 | Action item klien — gather data sesi berikutnya |

**Output sesi 2-3:** 2 alur priority dengan A.R.S.I. analysis + future state sketch.

### 4.3. Sesi 4 — Synthesis + Roadmap (90-min)

**Tujuan:** Sintesa 3 alur audit jadi roadmap implementasi + permission system tim.

**Pre-sesi:**
- Klien review 3 alur audit dari sesi 1-3 + identify 1 alur paling siap implement first

**Agenda 90-min:**

| Menit | Block |
|---|---|
| 0-15 | Recap 3 alur priority + future state sketch |
| 15-40 | Permission system design — siapa boleh pakai AI di alur mana, dengan boundary apa |
| 40-65 | Roadmap implementasi 4-8 minggu pasca-engagement (sequencing: alur 1 first, lalu 2, lalu 3) |
| 65-80 | Tools setup priority (kalau ada) — 2-3 tools paling kritikal |
| 80-90 | Action item — klien review draft deliverable yang saya kirim 7 hari kemudian |

**Output sesi 4:** Permission system + roadmap implementasi + tools priority.

### 4.4. Deliverable + Handover Sesi (post-sesi-4)

**Deliverable:** Dokumen 30-50 halaman PDF custom untuk klien:

| Section deliverable | Content |
|---|---|
| Executive summary | 1 halaman — masalah, A.R.S.I. analysis, key recommendation, roadmap timeline |
| Alur audit (1-3) | 6-12 halaman — per alur: current state diagram, pain point, A.R.S.I. analysis, future state diagram |
| Permission system | 4-6 halaman — SOP tim: siapa boleh pakai AI, untuk apa, dengan boundary apa |
| Roadmap implementasi | 4-6 halaman — sequencing 8-12 minggu post-engagement, milestone, owner per fase |
| Tools setup priority | 2-3 halaman — 2-3 tools yang paling kritikal + setup spec |
| FAQ + edge cases | 2-3 halaman — pertanyaan yang muncul + adjustment per konteks |
| Appendix | Templates + worksheet untuk tim klien implement |

**Turnaround time deliverable:** 5-7 hari kerja post-sesi-4.

**Handover sesi (60-min):**
- Walkthrough deliverable dengan klien (30-min)
- Q&A klien (15-min)
- Action plan 30-hari pasca-handover (10-min)
- Closing + post-engagement upsell soft mention (5-min — Section 9)

**SACRED:** Recording handover sesi = OK dengan consent klien (untuk archive klien). Recording sesi 1-4 diagnostic = klien only, saya tidak retain.

### 4.5. Large Variant (6-Sesi, Rp 100JT)

Untuk tim 30-50 orang cross-functional, 4 sesi tidak cukup. Variant 6-sesi:

| Sesi | Format | Durasi |
|---|---|---|

```
Tidak ada termination ad-hoc tanpa written notice.
```

### 7.2. Standard Clauses

#### Clause 6: Scope Boundary Explicit

```
Scope terbatas pada:
- [N] sesi diagnostic 90-min via Zoom (jadwal sesuai mutual agreement)
- 1 deliverable PDF custom ~[N] halaman
- 1 handover sesi 60-min

Yang TIDAK termasuk:
- Implementation hands-on (deploy tools, hire tim, dll)
- Ongoing maintenance post-handover
- Tools subscription cost (klien yang procure)
- Training delivery ke tim >5 orang
- Compliance audit / legal review
- Custom AI model development

Untuk item di atas, refer ke vendor specialized atau add-on terpisah
dengan fee terpisah.
```

#### Clause 7: Recording & Documentation

```
Sesi diagnostic + handover dapat direcord dengan consent kedua
pihak. Recording menjadi milik klien sepenuhnya post-engagement.

Service Provider working notes (Notion private workspace):
- Akses internal Service Provider only
- Anonimisasi composite untuk reference 12 bulan post-engagement
- Purge full data 12 bulan post-engagement (kecuali NDA mensyarakatkan
  earlier purge)

Diagram + sketsa Miro: Klien retain ownership transfer post-engagement.
```

### 7.3. Edge Case Clauses

#### Clause 8: Force Majeure
Standard force majeure (bencana alam, pandemi, perubahan regulasi
fundamental). Engagement defer atau terminate by mutual agreement.

#### Clause 9: Indemnification (Limited)
Service Provider tidak liable untuk consequence implementasi klien
post-handover. Deliverable = recommendation; eksekusi = klien
responsibility.

Maximum liability: total fee yang sudah dibayar klien. Tidak ada
liability untuk consequential damages, lost profits, dll.

#### Clause 10: Jurisdiction
Hukum Indonesia. Dispute resolution: BANI (Badan Arbitrase Nasional
Indonesia) atau pengadilan Jakarta Pusat (mutual choice).

## Section 8 — Delivery Operational

### 8.1. Pre-Kickoff Setup (Post-Contract Signing)

| T+ jam | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| T+0 | Counter-sign contract diterima klien | Klien |
| T+24 | 50% upfront invoice received | Klien |
| T+24 | Lunas confirmation email + Notion workspace setup trigger | Anda |
| T+72 | Notion workspace klien ready: 4 sesi folder + worksheet template + pre-cohort form to klien | Anda + VA |
| T+72 | Email klien: "Notion ready, pre-cohort form deadline 5 hari" | Anda |
| T+5 hari | Klien isi pre-cohort form | Klien |
| T+5-7 hari | Saya review form, prep sesi 1 agenda | Anda |
| T+7 hari | Sesi 1 kickoff (Senin atau Selasa default) | Mutual |

**SLA:** Maksimum 7 hari kerja dari payment receipt ke kickoff sesi 1.

### 8.2. Sesi 1-4 Cadence

**Default cadence:** 1 sesi/minggu (Senin atau Selasa 14:00-15:30 WIB default).

**Adjustment options:**

| Klien preference | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Tim klien butuh implementasi gradual antar-sesi | 1 sesi per 2 minggu (timeline jadi 8-12 minggu, no extra fee) |
| Klien rush timeline | 2 sesi per minggu (timeline jadi 4-5 minggu, +20% premium kalau total <4 minggu) |
| Klien holiday/event conflict | Reschedule 1× OK, 2× = case-by-case, 3× = case-by-case dengan kemungkinan timeline extension |

**SACRED:** TIDAK ada sesi >2/minggu walaupun klien push (Anti-Burnout S-4 — kolab schedule rule).

### 8.3. Inter-Sesi Support

**Channel komunikasi:**
- **Notion comment thread:** Default async untuk Q&A, klarifikasi, follow-up action item. Saya respon 1 hari kerja.
- **Email:** Untuk formal request (perubahan scope, timeline adjustment, deliverable feedback). Respon 1-2 hari kerja.
- **WA group:** EMERGENCY ONLY (mis: sesi cancel mendadak, force majeure). Tidak untuk daily Q&A.

**Working hours:** Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00 WIB. Off weekend (Anti-Burnout S-3 SACRED).

**Anti-pattern:**
- Klien push WA daily Q&A → redirect ke Notion comment
- Klien expect <1 jam response time → set expectation 1 hari kerja default
- Klien ad-hoc request "quick call sebentar" → schedule via Calendly, bukan ad-hoc

### 8.4. Quality Control SOP (Pre-Deliverable)

**Sebelum kirim deliverable PDF ke klien:**

| Step | Apa yang dicek |
|---|---|
| 1. Self-review draft (saya) | Voice consistency + factual accuracy + alur logic |
| 2. Recording cross-reference | Setiap claim di deliverable bisa di-trace ke recording sesi 1-4 |
| 3. Anonimisasi audit (CO-7) | Pastikan tidak ada PII tim klien yang slip ke deliverable (nama orang, email internal, phone, dll) |
| 4. Designer review (kalau ada) | Visual consistency Light theme + Playfair+Inter |
| 5. Spell check + grammar | Bahasa Indonesia formal — paste ke spell checker |
| 6. Cross-ref methodology | A.R.S.I. mention konsisten + signature phrase muncul minimum 1× |
| 7. Final read-through | 1-jam break + read fresh — catch yang missed di first review |

**Turnaround time deliverable:** 5-7 hari kerja post-sesi-4 (Standard). 7-10 hari kerja (Large variant). NO same-week delivery — quality > speed.

### 8.5. Handover Ritual

**Handover sesi (60-min, default Selasa atau Kamis 14:00-15:00 WIB):**

| Menit | Block |
|---|---|
| 0-5 | Welcome + recap engagement journey 6-8 minggu |
| 5-30 | Walkthrough deliverable section-by-section + Q&A klarifikasi |
| 30-45 | Action plan 30-hari pasca-handover (klien yang lead) — saya validate + suggest priority |
| 45-55 | Q&A klien tambahan + edge cases yang muncul saat reading deliverable |
| 55-60 | Closing + soft mention upsell path (Section 9) — TIDAK push, hanya inform options |

**SACRED:** TIDAK push annual retainer atau phase 2 di handover sesi (Section 9.7 anti-churn rule). Soft mention only.

**Post-handover:**
- 50% final invoice sent dalam 24 jam post-handover
- Access Notion workspace klien tetap aktif 30 hari (untuk klien re-reference deliverable atau worksheet)
- Email follow-up D+30: "Bagaimana progress 30-hari pasca-handover?" → trigger upsell organic kalau klien stuck atau butuh next step

### 8.6. Post-Engagement Protocol

**D+30 days post-handover:**
- Notion workspace access REVOKE
- Working notes saya = anonimisasi composite untuk reference
- Recording handover = klien retain, saya purge

**D+12 months post-handover:**
- Working notes saya = full purge (NDA atau policy)
- Aggregate insight (anonimisasi composite) dipakai untuk konten editorial generic OK

**Long-term:**
- Klien tetap dapat Q&A reasonable (1-2 email/tahun) — nurture goodwill
- Kalau klien butuh phase 2 atau ongoing = upsell path Section 9

### 8.7. Energy Management Plan untuk Anda

(Anchor `ANTI-BURNOUT-PROTOCOL.md` §S-4 + §7 launch survival adapt corporate.)

**Default cap:**
- Max 1 sesi diagnostic / hari (90-min sesi + 30-min prep + 60-min post-sesi notes = 3 jam minimum)
- Buffer 2 jam post-sesi sebelum next meeting/work
- 1 hari OFF total post-handover (full decompression)

**Cap simultaneous engagement:**
- Bulan 4-5 (build capacity): 2 engagement aktif max
- Bulan 6-12 (steady state): 3 engagement aktif max (mix tier OK)
- NEVER: 4+ engagement aktif simultaneous = quality drop + Anti-Burnout RED tier risk

**Energy check trigger:**
- Pre-kickoff: Energy audit GREEN required (skor ≥21)
- Mid-engagement: Weekly energy check
- Post-handover: 3-day mini-sabbatical (Anti-Burnout §10 sabbatical cadence)

## Section 9 — Post-Engagement Upsell Path

### 9.1. Annual Retainer

**Spec:**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Rp 75-150JT/tahun (small/medium tier basis) |
| **Format** | 4 quarterly check-in 90-min via Zoom + email Q&A unlimited (24-jam SLA) + priority access (skip waitlist untuk corporate audit phase 2) |
| **Cap** | Max 5 retainer klien simultaneous |
| **Trigger** | Klien proactive ask post-handover atau 30-day check-in |

**Best fit:** Klien yang sudah selesai corporate audit awal + butuh ongoing accountability + tidak siap commit ke corporate audit phase 2.

### 9.2. Quarterly Check-In (Standalone)

**Spec:**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Rp 15-25JT/quarter |
| **Format** | 1-on-1 sesi 90-min via Zoom + mini-deliverable update (5-10 halaman) |
| **Cadence** | Per kuartal, fleksibel 3-4 bulan gap |

**Best fit:** Klien yang inginkan periodic accountability tanpa commitment annual retainer.

### 9.3. Cohort Referral

**Trigger:** Klien punya tim manajer/senior yang belum punya konteks A.R.S.I. — bisa direkomen ke cohort A.R.S.I.

**Mekanisme:**
- Klien refer manajer/senior ke cohort
- Manajer dapat **priority slot** (skip queue waitlist)
- Manajer dapat **waiver discovery call** (klien sudah validate mereka sebagai qualified)
- TIDAK ada referral fee (anti-affiliate)

**Best fit:** Klien Medium/Large dengan tim manajer multi-level yang butuh up-skilling A.R.S.I.

### 9.4. Speaker Engagement

**Spec:**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Rp 25-50JT/event |
| **Format** | 60-90 min talk + Q&A 30-min, internal event klien (offline atau online) |
| **Topic** | A.R.S.I. methodology + case study anonimisasi (NOT case klien sendiri kecuali consent) |
| **Cap** | Max 1 speaker engagement/bulan (Anti-Burnout) |

**Best fit:** Klien yang organize internal AI conference / town hall / leadership summit dan ingin saya keynote.

### 9.5. Phase 2 Audit

**Spec:**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Pricing** | Rp 25-50JT (lower tier vs phase 1 — context already known) |
| **Format** | 4 sesi diagnostic (similar phase 1) tetapi fokus phase 2: alur kerja yang BELUM di-audit di phase 1 atau scaling beyond original tim |
| **Trigger** | Post 6-bulan implementasi phase 1 + klien butuh extension scope |

**Best fit:** Klien yang phase 1 sudah selesai implementasi 6 bulan + ada alur baru atau divisi baru yang butuh audit.

### 9.6. Trigger Upsell — Kapan Push (atau TIDAK Push)

**OK untuk soft mention:**
- Handover sesi (60-min) — closing 5-min slot mention options (CO-3.7 anti-churn — soft only)
- 30-day check-in email post-handover
- Klien proactive ask

**TIDAK OK push:**
- Mid-engagement sebelum handover
- Setiap touchpoint klien (over-pitch fatigue)
- Klien yang explicitly decline upsell di handover

### 9.7. Anti-Churn Rule (Defer Upsell ke 30-Day Check-In)

**SACRED:** Tidak push upsell di handover sesi (di luar soft 5-min mention).

**Rationale:** Klien yang baru selesai engagement = fatigue + processing deliverable. Push upsell di handover = trust break + perception "saya cuma cari uang". Defer ke 30-day check-in = klien sudah sempat eksperimen, kalau butuh next step, mereka yang minta.

**30-day check-in email format:**

```
Subject: 30 hari post-engagement — bagaimana progress?

Halo [Nama],

30 hari pasca-handover. Quick check-in:

1. Apakah Anda sudah mulai implement roadmap 30-hari yang kita
   diskusi di handover?

2. Ada blocker yang muncul yang TIDAK ter-cover di deliverable?

3. Apakah ada update dari tim yang Anda ingin share (positif atau
   negatif)?

Tidak ada commitment dari reply — saya cuma curious dan ingin
support kalau ada yang bisa saya bantu.

Kalau Anda inginkan support struktural berkelanjutan, saya punya
beberapa option:
- Annual retainer (4× quarterly check-in + email Q&A)
- Quarterly check-in standalone
- Phase 2 audit untuk alur baru atau scaling

Tidak ada urgency — opsi ini ada kalau Anda inginkan, tidak kalau
tidak inginkan.

Salam,
Arif
```

**Send timing:** D+30 hari post-handover, hari kerja pagi (Selasa atau Rabu 09:00 WIB).
