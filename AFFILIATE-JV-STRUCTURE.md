## FILOSOFI PARTNERSHIP

Partnership = **leverage trust dari brand lain** untuk audience yang sudah qualified, bukan jalan pintas brand-building. Anda BUKAN partner siapapun yang nawarin — Anda partner orang yang audience-nya match brand "AI Architect" Indonesia.

**3 Sacred rules sebelum partnership:**

1. **JANGAN partnership untuk reach saja** — kalau audience partner tidak match brand AI Architect, conversion = 0% + brand consistency rusak
2. **JANGAN sakralkan core IP di affiliate program** — buku tier 1-3 OK affiliate, tapi cohort + corporate audit = direct only (high-touch + screening required)
3. **JANGAN sign exclusive deal early** — Bulan 4-6 = explore + validate, exclusive lock-in baru relevant Bulan 9+ kalau ada partner top-tier

**Goal partnership:**
- Affiliate buku: 20-30% sales boost dari channel partner
- JV cohort: 1-2 cohort co-launch dengan brand komplementer (Bulan 9-12)
- Brand reach expansion ke 3-5 niche komplementer (mis. productivity, founder, B2B Indonesia)

**Apa yang TIDAK boleh di-partner:**
- Buku core IP (royalty 100% Anda, tidak ghostwrite)
- Cohort decision (curriculum, peserta cap, harga, refund) = sacred
- Corporate audit engagement (high-touch only Anda)
- Brand voice + manifesto (no co-branded watered-down version)

## PROGRAM 1 — AFFILIATE BUKU

### Struktur

| Aspek | Spesifikasi |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility partner** | Akun yang Anda kenal personally OR sudah minimum 3 bulan engage konten Anda secara substantive (not just like) |
| **Komisi** | 10% flat dari harga buku tier 1-3 (bukan diskon ke pembeli) |
| **Cookie duration** | 30 hari (industry standard, fair untuk content-driven affiliate) |
| **Payout** | Bulanan, akhir bulan, transfer (kalau minimum Rp 100K), atau cumulative kalau di bawah Rp 100K |
| **Tracking** | Custom URL `arifb.id/buku?ref=[partner-handle]` (pakai Lynk.id Pro tier saat trigger M3 atau Bitly) |
| **Tier** | 1 tier flat (no tiered commission untuk simplicity Day 1) |
| **Aktivasi** | Bulan 4 awal (post-buku-launch settle 4 minggu, baseline conversion data ada) |

### Kriteria partner

**ACCEPT:**
- Audience profesional Indonesia 30-45 tahun (overlap target ICP)
- Brand voice formal / professional (bukan hype-bro)
- Follower aktif minimum 1000 (engagement rate minimum 3%)
- Konten yang relate ke productivity / sistem / decision-making / B2B
- History clean (tidak pernah dispute / scam komplain)

**REJECT:**
- Audience demografis tidak match (mis. teen / general consumer)
- Voice clickbait / "10 tools rahasia" / hype-bro pattern
- Engagement rate di bawah 2% (akun fake follower atau audience tidak engaged)
- Sebelumnya pernah promote conflict produk (mis. course "AI rahasia 10 hari miliarder")
- Konten promote pernah breach brand voice formal

### Outreach template (untuk partner inbound)

**Saat ada akun yang DM minta affiliate:**

```

3 quick check:

1. Bagaimana audience Anda (umur, role, industri primer)?
2. 1-2 contoh konten Anda yang most engaged 3 bulan terakhir (link)?
3. Kenapa Anda interested promote buku saya specifically (bukan jualan, mau tau perspektif Anda)?

Kalau match, saya kirim detail program (10% komisi, 30-hari cookie, custom tracking URL).

— Arif
```

### Outreach template (untuk Anda reach out partner ideal)

**Saat Anda spot akun yang ideal partner:**

```

Saya Arif Budiman, founder PROMIND-NEUROLINK, penulis buku THE AI ARCHITECT.

Saya mengikuti konten Anda 2-3 bulan terakhir, terutama soal {1 topik spesifik mereka}. Audience Anda overlap dengan ICP saya (founder & profesional Indonesia yang serius soal sistem, bukan sekadar tools).

Buku saya rilis Mgg 7 [tanggal]. Setelah launch settle, saya buka program affiliate selektif untuk 5-10 partner pertama.

Kalau Anda interested explore — bukan commit, hanya explore — reply email ini dengan "DETAIL". Saya kirim spec program + sample copy yang Anda bisa adaptasi.

— Arif
```

### Asset bantuan untuk affiliate

Setelah partner accept, Anda kasih:
- 5 hook angle untuk post promote (variasi format Threads / IG / LinkedIn)
- 2 sample caption (Indonesian + bahasa Inggris kalau partner audience bilingual)
- 1 image/visual quote dari buku (Canva editable)
- Custom tracking URL `arifb.id/buku?ref=[partner-handle]`
- 1 page brand voice quick guide (so partner tidak misrepresent)

### Anti-abuse rules

- Partner spam DM list mereka — terdeteksi via complaint, instant terminate
- Partner pakai bot/buy comment untuk inflate buku promo post — instant terminate, no payout
- Partner bundle buku dengan course mereka tanpa permission — breach contract
- Partner discount harga buku dari komisi mereka (mis. "beli via saya, Anda dapat Rp 50K off") — destroy harga buku integrity, terminate
- Partner yang violate aturan = remove dari list, public announcement (no name shame, tapi ada flag di program FAQ)

## PROGRAM 2 — JV COHORT (BULAN 9-12)

### Struktur (high-level, detail di Bulan 8 saat data Q2-Q3 cukup)

| Aspek | Spesifikasi (draft) |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Cohort co-launch dengan 1 partner brand komplementer |
| **Eligibility partner** | Brand established (1 founder yang Anda respect, audience minimum 10K engaged, history co-launch sukses) |
| **Revenue split** | 70/30 atau 60/40 (Anda mayor karena curriculum + delivery) |
| **Curriculum** | 50% A.R.S.I. core (Anda) + 50% complementary topic dari partner (mis. operations, sales, hiring) |
| **Cap** | 30 peserta (vs cohort solo 20) |
| **Schedule** | Bulan 9-10 atau Bulan 10-11 (timing depend Q3 data) |

### Kriteria JV partner

**ACCEPT:**
- Brand established (audience minimum 10K profesional)
- Curriculum komplementer (NOT overlap dengan A.R.S.I. core, tapi enhance)
- Founder yang Anda respect personally + brand voice align
- History delivery cohort/course berkualitas (completion rate minimum 85%, testimonial verified)
- Mutual respect dynamic (50/50 di decision making, walau revenue bisa weighted)

**REJECT:**
- Brand overlap dengan A.R.S.I. core (mis. partner yang juga teach AI prompting framework)
- Founder dengan history red flag (controversial post, tone-deaf moments, audience drama)
- "Cohort" yang sebenarnya disguised webinar 2 hari (tidak true cohort delivery)
- Revenue pressure terlalu agresif ("kita bisa dapat 100 peserta!" = cap discipline broken)

### Decision matrix JV

| Factor | Weight | Score 1-10 (per partner) |
|---|---|---|
| Audience overlap (qualified) | 30% | ? |
| Brand voice alignment | 25% | ? |
| Curriculum complementarity | 20% | ? |
| Founder track record | 15% | ? |
| Revenue split fairness | 10% | ? |

**Decision rule:**
- Score minimum 80% = proceed dengan trial conversation
- Score 60-80% = explore tapi not commit
- Score di bawah 60% = polite decline

## TRACKING & PAYOUT (LYNK.ID PRO REQUIRED)

### Tracking setup

- **Trigger upgrade Lynk.id Pro:** Saat M3 trigger aktif (Day 60 / Mgg 6 / 3+ conv-week, per Wave 2.1 decision lock)
- **Custom URL feature** Lynk.id Pro = enable per-partner tracking
- **Backup:** Bitly free tier untuk pre-Pro setup (5 link kustom free)
- **Reporting:** Sheet `AFFILIATE-TRACKING` di Sheets tab baru, manual entry monthly

### Payout flow

1. End of month: compile sales attributed via tracking URL
2. Calculate komisi 10% × harga tier yang terjual
3. Verify dengan Lynk.id payment record (cross-check)
4. Transfer ke partner (transfer bank Indonesia, atau Wise kalau partner luar)
5. Email partner detail breakdown + CSV (transparent)

### Audit per quarter

- Q1 review: top 3 partner produktif, bottom 3 yang inactive
- Q2 review: terminate inactive, recruit 2-3 partner baru kalau slot tersedia
- Q3 review: consider tiered commission structure kalau ada partner outlier (sales attribution minimum 30%)
- Q4 review: prepare YoY analysis untuk Q1 next year

## ANTI-PATTERN PARTNERSHIP

1. Auto-accept setiap akun yang DM minta affiliate — degraded program quality, brand dilution
2. Negotiate komisi naik untuk partner yang nggak deliver — set expectation, stick to it
3. JV cohort dengan 3+ partner sekaligus — chaos, voice dilution, cap discipline broken
4. Sign exclusive partner Bulan 4-6 — terlalu cepat, lock out future better partner
5. Co-brand dengan brand yang voice mismatch — audience Anda bingung, trust drop

---

## CATATAN PENUTUP

Partnership = **selektif, slow burn, brand-aligned**. Bulan 4-6 = test affiliate buku dengan 5-10 partner, validate. Bulan 9-12 = first JV cohort co-launch (kalau data Q2-Q3 support).

Kalau partnership done right:
- 20-30% sales boost dari channel
- Brand reach expand ke niche komplementer
- Peer network founder yang compound trust

Kalau partnership done wrong:
- Brand dilution
- Audience confusion
- Drama yang habiskan energi 3-6 bulan

Selektif > scale. Selalu.
